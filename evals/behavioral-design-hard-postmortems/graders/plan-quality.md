PASS only if ALL four hold:

1. Before recommending interventions, the reply diagnoses why post-mortems are skipped or late, naming at least one concrete barrier (for example no protected time or slot, a blank-page / heavy template, unclear owner, fear of blame) rather than assuming engineers do not care.
2. It advises against tying completion to the performance review (or substantially reworks it), and gives a concrete mechanism for why it backfires: box-ticking / low-quality write-ups, crowding out intrinsic motivation, or blame fear that leads to incidents being under-declared or downgraded.
3. Its top-ranked intervention is an ease or default lever (for example the post-mortem doc auto-created and pre-filled from the incident timeline, an owner auto-assigned, a review meeting scheduled by default), not a mandate, incentive, or awareness campaign.
4. It gives a measurement plan with a metric drawn from a system of record (incident tracker / doc tool, e.g. share of incidents with a post-mortem within N days), a baseline, and either a comparison design (staggered rollout by team, pilot vs. comparison) or a planned re-measure after several weeks.

FAIL if any of the four is missing, or if the reply's plan implements the performance-review link as proposed.
